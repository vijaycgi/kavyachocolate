# Home

**Mode:** Persuade · **Scope:** `/` storefront homepage

## Audience and job

First-time shoppers in India and abroad who want real bean-to-bar chocolate and need to believe the ingredients before buying. Job: understand what makes these four bars different, pick one, and start an order in under three minutes.

## Success and constraints

Success: the visitor can name the four bars and the promise ("real cocoa beans, minimal ingredients") after one viewport, and reaches Shop or a product page without hunting. Must stay honest: placeholder prices and photography are labeled; jaggery is never called sugar-free; no invented reviews, certifications, or health claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A chocolate atelier counter, not a Shopify grid: cream paper, burgundy lacquer, ruled editorial type, and bars presented like specimens with tasting notes. Refuses the centered-hero + three-icon-cards + review-carousel default.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm cream paper `#F6EFE3` with a fine grain; burgundy `#75212F` as a structural field, not a sprinkle; cocoa ink `#24140F`; brass hairlines `#B08A57`; sharp 2px corners; Sen for display, UI, body and money, with weight and case carrying hierarchy and no italic; oversized still-life plates with one soft shadow.

**STORY:** These are four honest bars made from real cocoa beans. The visitor learns the ingredient logic, sees the craft, chooses a bar, adds it to the bag, and orders without a payment step.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** At 1440px: a burgundy announcement rule; cream header with the logo centered and thin rules; a 58/42 split hero — left an oversized display headline and one supporting paragraph with a handwritten ingredient note and two actions; right a burgundy plate holding the chocolate still life and one floating tasting card; a ruled proof strip closes the viewport.

**FORM:** Position 1 of 7 — the confectionery counter / specimen ledger. seed: none (brief-pinned).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
